Setup

  1. Onshape only looks for a 3D mouse when it believes it runs on Windows or macOS, which a tiny browser extension fixes. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and select ~/.local/share/spacemouse-onshape/extension. In Firefox, or with Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey, install the userscript instead.
  2. Open or reload an Onshape document, click into the 3D view, and move the cap.
  3. Move the cap here: the bars show the motion after the dead zone, in the screen's frame. Pushing right should fill pan-x positively, lifting should fill pan-y positively, and pulling toward you should fill zoom positively.
